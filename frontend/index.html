Common questions

How accurate is the estimate?

It uses ten-year average rainfall for your pin and standard runoff factors. Real rainfall changes from year to year, so treat the figures as a guide, not a guarantee.

Is harvested water safe to drink?

Not without proper treatment. Use it for flushing, washing, gardening and recharge unless you add filtration and disinfection designed for drinking water.

Are the prices exact?

No. They come from rough rates kept in the app's data file. Get quotes from local suppliers before you build.

Why does the voice button not work?

Voice input relies on your browser's speech recognition. It works best in Chrome and Edge, needs microphone permission and an internet connection.
